Hustle Guild

We are a focused guild built on discipline, progression, and community. Our members team up for weekly goals, support each other with guides and mentoring, and keep improving together.

What We Stand For

Consistency

Daily progress beats random bursts. We value players who show up and help the group move forward.

Teamwork

From boss runs to event prep, we coordinate and communicate so everyone benefits.

Mastery

We optimize builds, share class knowledge, and practice strategy to play at a high level.

Guild Activities

  • Weekly group runs and progression sessions
  • Build coaching and class-specific help
  • Community events and friendly competitions

We also follow the classic spirit highlighted on the TimelessMS site: community-first gameplay, long-term progression, and a nostalgic MMORPG experience. Visit timelessms.github.io/site/index.html for reference.
